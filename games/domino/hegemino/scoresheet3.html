Hegemino Score Sheets

Hegemino is my domino game for 1–4 players, along the lines of Kingdomino.

This page will calculate scores for three players. See the single scoresheet page for links for other player counts.

In the tables below, you may omit one of pip squares, wild squares, and all squares, depending on which ones you prefer to count, and the remaining value will be calculated. If you need to change a value later, edit pip squares or wild squares.
