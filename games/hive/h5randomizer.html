Hive in Five Randomizer

Hive in Five is a solo/puzzle mode for the abstract strategy game Hive. The goal is to surround the black queen in exactly 5 moves (or multiples thereof), moving each type of black piece only once. (Use the white pieces to keep track of your moves.)
