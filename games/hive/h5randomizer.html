Hive in Five Randomizer

Hive in Five is an award-winning solo/puzzle mode for the award-winning abstract strategy game Hive. The goal is to surround the black queen in exactly 5 moves (or multiples thereof), moving each type of piece only once. Use the white pieces to keep track of your moves.
