Setup

Each player receives:

  • a set of 6 aces
  • a set of 6 suit tokens
  • a hand of 4 base cards and 1 extended deck card

 

The markets are set up with:

  • six base deck cards, discarding any 7s, 8s, or 9s
  • three extended deck cards, discarding any Courts or Excuses
  • optionally, six eyeballs on a price sheet
