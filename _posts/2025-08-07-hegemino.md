Once again, I haven't blogged much but I have been up to stuff.  I've been playing and programming some Decktet and other games at [Abstract Play](https://play.abstractplay.com).  Rather than devise a [Piecepack](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/2860/piecepack) game as I was thinking last time, I devised a domino game, [Hegemino](/games/domino/hegemino/).  It's the easy-to-play Kingdomino-style game that [Personimo](/games/decktet/personimo/) never really was.
