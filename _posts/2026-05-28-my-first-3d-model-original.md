You may recall I got a 3D printer a month ago, and today I published my first from-scratch model, which I pieced together from basic shapes in TinkerCad.

We were using a soap alligator on a string to hold the shower door open, but the toddler likes to play with it and when he pulled it off the door the string dissolved into nylon fuzz.  Rather than find a new string, I decided to go with [a less tempting solution](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2859308-shower-door-prop):  a door prop that hangs on the shower door handle.
