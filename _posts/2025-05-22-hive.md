I haven't blogged much but I have been up to some stuff.  I've tweaked the draft rules to [Darcana](/games/decktet/darcana/), my reimplementation of Dectana, a bit.  I've been playing some Decktet and other games at [Abstract Play](https://play.abstractplay.com), and I'm hoping to find or devise an abstract [Piecepack](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/2860/piecepack) game (besides [Alien City](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/20623/alien-city)).

Lately I've been playing a little [Hive](/games/hive/).  I was inspired to implement a [randomizer](/games/hive/h5randomizer.html) for the solo Hive puzzle [Hive in Five](/games/hive/hiveIn5.html), and of course to add my own [algebraic notation for Hive](/games/hive/algebraicNotation.html) to the long list of [proposed Hive notation systems](/games/hive/notation.html).

Maybe I'll get to play one of the games at [Balticon](https://www.balticon.org) this weekend.
